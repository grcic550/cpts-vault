---
title: "Pentest Mindset"
---

The exam rewards **methodical enumeration and note-taking**, not clever one-liners. Enumerate more than you think you need to, and write down everything as you go.

<Note>
**Rules of thumb**
- Enumeration never really stops — every new credential or host restarts it.
- If you're stuck, you missed something. Re-enumerate the last thing you touched.
- One finding at a time; log the command, the output, and the proof before moving on.
- Screenshot proof (`whoami`, `hostname`, flag) the moment you land it.
</Note>

## The loop

1. **Enumerate** the target/service exhaustively.
2. **Identify** a misconfiguration, weak credential, or known vuln.
3. **Exploit** to get a foothold (least effort first).
4. **Loot** — creds, hashes, config, sensitive files.
5. **Escalate** locally, then **pivot** and repeat from step 1.

<Tip>
Keep a running "creds & hosts" table. In AD especially, every password sprays across every host and restarts the whole loop.
</Tip>
