## Current operation: Test one permitted candidate against a bounded account list

**Run in:** Windows PowerShell. **Requires:** Known effective lockout policies, account state and a permitted test window/rate; the module and supplied user list must match the intended domain.

```powershell
Import-Module .\DomainPasswordSpray.ps1
Invoke-DomainPasswordSpray -UserList users.txt -Password 'PASSWORD_CANDIDATE' -OutFile spray-results.txt
```

**Read the result:** Interpret authentication outcomes together with account state and the effective lockout policy.

**If Uniform failures or lockouts appear during the test:** Policy discovery is a prerequisite; one domain policy may not describe fine-grained policies for every account. Avoid guessing from missing output.

**Next check:** Stop to resolve policy, identity and connectivity; validate any reported success with the intended service rather than assuming broader access.

**Scope of these examples:** The operations above provide the current starting checks. The complete earlier guide, variants and lab transcripts remain below; they retain their original assumptions.
