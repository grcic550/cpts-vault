## Invoke-DomainPasswordSpray: choose the operation and read the result

### What the result establishes

Interpret authentication outcomes together with account state and the effective lockout policy.

### Prerequisites and common failures

Policy discovery is a prerequisite; one domain policy may not describe fine-grained policies for every account. Avoid guessing from missing output.
