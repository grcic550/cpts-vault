## arp: choose the operation and read the result

Commands and examples collected from your arp notes.

### What the result establishes

Associate neighbor entries with the local link and interface, checking whether the entry is dynamic or stale.

### Prerequisites and common failures

ARP only describes local-layer adjacency for IPv4; it is not a discovery method for arbitrary routed subnets.
