## Current operation: Read managed-password material available to this principal

**Run in:** Kali, in the gMSADumper checkout. **Requires:** A gMSA deployment and an identity with the required managed-password retrieval right; ordinary metadata read access is separate.

```bash
python3 gMSADumper.py -u USER -p 'PASSWORD' -d DOMAIN_FQDN -l DC_FQDN
```

**Read the result:** Associate any returned managed-password material with its gMSA identity and the principal permitted to retrieve it.

**If The account is found but its password blob is unavailable:** Readable directory metadata is different from permission to retrieve managed-password data; inspect the relevant access configuration.

**Next check:** Inspect the configured reader principals and effective membership; retain the gMSA identity with any returned artifact.

**Scope of these examples:** The operations above provide the current starting checks. The complete earlier guide, variants and lab transcripts remain below; they retain their original assumptions.
