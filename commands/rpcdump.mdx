## rpcdump.py: choose the operation and read the result

Commands and examples collected from your rpcdump.py notes.

### What the result establishes

Read interface UUIDs and endpoint bindings as evidence of exposed RPC services.

### Prerequisites and common failures

An advertised endpoint does not prove an exploitable method or adequate caller permissions; dynamic RPC ports must also be reachable.
