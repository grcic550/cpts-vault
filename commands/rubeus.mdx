<Accordion title="Where it fits in a pentest">

### Where it fits in a pentest

**Pass the Ticket from Windows**

Context from the notebook: PtT is core  lateral movement  in an AD engagement. Typical chain: land on a box →  sekurlsa::ekeys / tickets  or  Rubeus dump  → grab a privileged user's TGT (or  asktgt  from their key) → inject →  Enter-PSSession / impacket  to the next host or the DC. It's preferred over PtH when the environment is Kerberos-only or you want to stay AES-native and quieter. It also chains from other attacks that  yield  tickets: Kerberoasting, AS-REP roasting, unconstrained delegation, and  Pass the Certificate  (PKINIT → TGT).

```text
Rubeus dump
```
