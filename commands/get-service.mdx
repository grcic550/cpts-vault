## Current operation: Inspect one service's status and startup mode

**Run in:** Windows PowerShell. **Requires:** A real service name and permission to read the service manager.

```powershell
Get-Service -Name 'SERVICE_NAME' | Format-List Name,DisplayName,Status,StartType
```

**Read the result:** Inspect state and correlate an interesting service with its executable path and execution identity.

**If The service is stopped or its display name differs from its service name:** Get-Service alone does not show every security property; service-control ACLs and filesystem permissions require separate checks.

**Next check:** Resolve the actual service name and query executable/account settings with Get-CimInstance or sc.exe qc before testing permissions.

**Scope of these examples:** The operations above provide the current starting checks. The complete earlier guide, variants and lab transcripts remain below; they retain their original assumptions.
