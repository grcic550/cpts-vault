## ticketer.py: choose the operation and read the result

Commands and examples collected from your ticketer.py notes.

### What the result establishes

Inspect the ticket principal, realm, service, lifetime and encryption context before selecting it in a client.

### Prerequisites and common failures

Forged-ticket exercises require the correct key material and domain identifiers; a generated cache alone proves no access.
