## Current operation: Collect Group Policy findings for review

**Run in:** Windows cmd.exe. **Requires:** The Group3r executable, domain access and an existing writable output directory.

```console
group3r.exe -f C:\Temp\group3r.log
```

**Read the result:** Trace each GPO finding to its linked scope, filtering and affected objects before assuming it applies to a target.

**If A finding is in a GPO that does not apply to the chosen host:** Readable policy settings do not establish edit permission, and an edited GPO need not apply immediately or to every host.

**Next check:** Check links, filtering and scope, then inspect relevant policy files and permissions on the affected objects.
