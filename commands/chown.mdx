## Current operation: Change ownership of one controlled lab file

**Run in:** Linux lab host. **Requires:** A disposable lab file, existing user/group and authority to change ownership; record its original numeric owner/group first.

```bash
sudo chown LAB_USER:LAB_GROUP ./lab-file
ls -ln ./lab-file
```

**Read the result:** Verify owner and group on the exact object, including whether a symlink or its target is affected.

**If Ownership changes but the intended read/write access still fails:** Ownership changes usually require privilege and can alter special permission bits; record the original state first.

**Next check:** Inspect mode bits, ACLs and mount restrictions; restore the recorded owner/group after testing instead of recursively changing an application tree.

**Scope of these examples:** The operations above provide the current starting checks. The complete earlier guide, variants and lab transcripts remain below; they retain their original assumptions.
