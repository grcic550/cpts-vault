## chown: choose the operation and read the result

Commands and examples collected from your chown notes.

### What the result establishes

Verify owner and group on the exact object, including whether a symlink or its target is affected.

### Prerequisites and common failures

Ownership changes usually require privilege and can alter special permission bits; record the original state first.
