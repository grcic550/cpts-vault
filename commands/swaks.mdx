## swaks: choose the operation and read the result

Commands and examples collected from your swaks notes.

### What the result establishes

Read the SMTP dialogue stage by stage: connection, EHLO, TLS, authentication, sender, recipient and message acceptance.

### Prerequisites and common failures

Recipient acceptance is not proof of mailbox delivery; server policy can reject or reroute a message after an earlier successful command.
