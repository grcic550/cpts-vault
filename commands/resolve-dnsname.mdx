## Commands

**Windows PowerShell**

```powershell
Resolve-DnsName -Name MS01 -Type A
Resolve-DnsName -Name MS01.DOMAIN -Server DC_IP -Type A
(Resolve-DnsName -Name MS01.DOMAIN -Server DC_IP -Type A).IPAddress
```

Replace DOMAIN and DC_IP with the lab domain and its DNS/DC address.
