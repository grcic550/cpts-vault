## Current operation: Map registered service principals to accounts

**Run in:** Windows cmd.exe. **Requires:** Directory connectivity and permission to query the domain's SPN registrations.

```console
setspn.exe -Q */*
```

**Read the result:** Map an SPN to the account that owns it; distinguish discovery queries from registration changes.

**If An SPN is duplicated or its hostname differs from the service used:** Duplicate SPNs, aliases and hostname differences affect Kerberos. A listed SPN does not establish the account's password strength.

**Next check:** Resolve the owning account and actual service hostname before choosing a Kerberos ticket request.

**Scope of these examples:** The operations above provide the current starting checks. The complete earlier guide, variants and lab transcripts remain below; they retain their original assumptions.

### 3. Request Tickets from Enumerated SPNs

**Kerberoasting from Windows**

```powershell

```
