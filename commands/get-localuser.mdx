## Current operation: List local accounts and enabled state

**Run in:** Windows PowerShell. **Requires:** The LocalAccounts module and a supported host; this enumerates local users, not local groups or domain users.

```powershell
Get-LocalUser | Select-Object Name,Enabled,LastLogon,SID
```

**Read the result:** Distinguish local account state and identifiers from similarly named domain accounts.

**If An account is enabled but cannot log in through the tested service:** Local-user enumeration is not domain enumeration; enabled status does not establish a valid password or remote logon right.

**Next check:** Check local group membership and that service's logon policy; do not equate account existence with a valid password.

**Scope of these examples:** The operations above provide the current starting checks. The complete earlier guide, variants and lab transcripts remain below; they retain their original assumptions.
