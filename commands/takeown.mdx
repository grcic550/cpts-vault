## Current operation: Take ownership of one controlled lab file

**Run in:** Elevated Windows cmd.exe. **Requires:** A disposable lab file, the required ownership privilege and a record of its original owner and ACL.

```console
takeown /f "C:\Lab\owned-test.txt"
```

**Read the result:** Record the original owner and inspect how ownership changes affect the subsequent permission operation.

**If Ownership changes but reading or writing is still denied:** Ownership is not identical to full file access, and taking ownership does not by itself rewrite every deny or access entry.

**Next check:** Inspect and deliberately adjust only the required DACL right if justified; ownership alone does not grant data access. Restore the recorded owner/ACL after the exercise.

**Scope of these examples:** The operations above provide the current starting checks. The complete earlier guide, variants and lab transcripts remain below; they retain their original assumptions.
