## Current operation: List available shadow copies in DiskShadow

**Run in:** Elevated Windows cmd.exe, then the DiskShadow prompt. **Requires:** Windows DiskShadow availability and administrative access; this sequence inventories existing snapshots.

```console
diskshadow
list shadows all
exit
```

**Read the result:** Understand the volume, snapshot context and exposed path before interpreting a shadow-copy result.

**If No snapshots are listed or the desired volume is absent:** Creating or exposing snapshots requires the relevant privileges and service support; a snapshot is not automatically a readable credential artifact.

**Next check:** Check volume and VSS context; creating/exposing a snapshot is a separate state-changing workflow and does not itself grant file-read rights.

**Scope of these examples:** The operations above provide the current starting checks. The complete earlier guide, variants and lab transcripts remain below; they retain their original assumptions.

**Windows Privilege Escalation: Full Checklist**

```powershell

vssadmin list shadows
```
