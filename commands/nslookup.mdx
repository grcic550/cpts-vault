## Current operation: Locate domain Kerberos service records

**Run in:** Kali or Windows command prompt. **Requires:** Domain DNS name and an appropriate internal resolver.

```bash
nslookup -type=SRV _kerberos._tcp.DOMAIN_FQDN DC_IP
```

**Read the result:** Confirm the selected DNS server and whether you asked for an address, reverse lookup or another record type.

**If No SRV records or an unexpected DNS server:** The default resolver may be inappropriate for the lab domain; an external DNS answer does not describe an internal AD zone.

**Next check:** Resolve the returned service hostnames and confirm reachability rather than treating the DNS response as authentication.

**Scope of these examples:** The operations above provide the current starting checks. The complete earlier guide, variants and lab transcripts remain below; they retain their original assumptions.
