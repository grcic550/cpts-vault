## Current operation: Inspect who may retrieve a gMSA password

**Run in:** Windows PowerShell. **Requires:** A gMSA identity and permission to read its directory configuration.

```powershell
Import-Module ActiveDirectory
Get-ADServiceAccount -Identity SERVICE_ACCOUNT -Properties PrincipalsAllowedToRetrieveManagedPassword,ServicePrincipalName
```

**Read the result:** Read service-account identity, SPNs and the principals permitted to retrieve managed passwords.

**If The gMSA is visible but its managed password is not retrievable:** Knowing a gMSA exists does not grant access to its managed secret; effective reader permissions must be verified.

**Next check:** Resolve the permitted-reader principals and your effective membership; existence and metadata access are insufficient.

**Scope of these examples:** The operations above provide the current starting checks. The complete earlier guide, variants and lab transcripts remain below; they retain their original assumptions.
