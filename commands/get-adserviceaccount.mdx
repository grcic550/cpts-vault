## List managed service accounts

**Windows PowerShell**

```powershell
Get-ADServiceAccount -Filter * -Properties PrincipalsAllowedToRetrieveManagedPassword | Select-Object Name,PrincipalsAllowedToRetrieveManagedPassword
```
