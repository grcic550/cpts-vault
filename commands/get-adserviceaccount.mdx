**Requires:** ActiveDirectory module and domain connectivity; viewing the object does not imply permission to retrieve its managed password.

## Inspect who may retrieve a gMSA password

**Run in:** Windows PowerShell. **Requires:** A gMSA identity and permission to read its directory configuration.

```powershell
Import-Module ActiveDirectory
Get-ADServiceAccount -Identity SERVICE_ACCOUNT -Properties PrincipalsAllowedToRetrieveManagedPassword,ServicePrincipalName
```

**What the result establishes:** Read service-account identity, SPNs and the principals permitted to retrieve managed passwords.

**Next check:** Resolve the permitted-reader principals and your effective membership; existence and metadata access are insufficient.

## List managed service accounts

**Windows PowerShell**

```powershell
Get-ADServiceAccount -Filter * -Properties PrincipalsAllowedToRetrieveManagedPassword | Select-Object Name,PrincipalsAllowedToRetrieveManagedPassword
```

## If it fails

| Symptom | Check |
| --- | --- |
| The gMSA is visible but its managed password is not retrievable | Knowing a gMSA exists does not grant access to its managed secret; effective reader permissions must be verified. |
