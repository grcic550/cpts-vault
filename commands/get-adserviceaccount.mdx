## Get-ADServiceAccount: choose the operation and read the result

List managed service accounts and their configured password-retrieval principals.

### What the result establishes

Read service-account identity, SPNs and the principals permitted to retrieve managed passwords.

### Prerequisites and common failures

Knowing a gMSA exists does not grant access to its managed secret; effective reader permissions must be verified.

ActiveDirectory module and domain connectivity; viewing the object does not imply permission to retrieve its managed password.

### Example 1: List managed service accounts

Run in: **Windows PowerShell**.

```powershell
Get-ADServiceAccount -Filter * -Properties PrincipalsAllowedToRetrieveManagedPassword | Select-Object Name,PrincipalsAllowedToRetrieveManagedPassword
```
