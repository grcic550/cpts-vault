## Record the network before adding a route

- Attacker address and VPN interface.
- Pivot's reachable address and internal interfaces.
- Internal subnet, target address, and domain-controller FQDN.
- Tunnel method, listening ports, and return path.
- Routes or listeners added during the lab, with cleanup notes.
