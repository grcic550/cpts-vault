## Keep it organized

1. Add a new lesson to its topic folder. Use `_Templates/Module_Note_Template.md` for a new module.
2. Keep raw pasted material in that topic's `Source Notes/` folder; give the organized note a descriptive title.
3. Record what you actually completed in the progress tracker. Keep personal box completion ticks in the local notes.
4. Use the AD learning log to capture the next lesson and unresolved questions.
5. The site update instructions are in [the site README](/downloads/readme-2507e7d8.txt). Generated pages are listed in `scripts/site-content.json`.

## Original material and recovery

The original files are preserved in `Archive/Original Vault 2026-09-07.tar.gz`. `Archive/original-files.json` records their original checksums; `Archive/moved-files.json` records where files were moved. No notes were discarded. The older `docs` copy is under `Archive/Mintlify Snapshot`; `cpts-next` and its related assets are under `Archive/Legacy Web Vault`.

The legacy visual project already lacked its required `index.html` and original vault HTML. Use the Mintlify site for the maintained web version.
