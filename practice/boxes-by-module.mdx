Choose a box that fits the CPTS modules you have studied. Track initial access separately from a full solve, then record what you can repeat independently.

[Open the interactive practice library](https://dashboard-aed21664.mintlify.site/practice/boxes-by-module) · [Written box catalogue](/practice/library-reference) · [AD lab guide](/lab-guide/reference)

## Your first visit

1. Open **My studied modules**. If accurate for you, choose **Select modules through Pivoting**, then adjust the checkboxes.
2. Enable **Include my current AD module as guided practice** while studying AD. This does not mark the module complete.
3. Keep **Active Directory** selected and choose **Ready or guided practice**. Start with the foothold goal; switch to full solve to see all remaining prerequisites.
4. Active, Forest and Sauna are useful starting candidates once their listed prerequisites fit. Use your current BloodHound guide alongside older walkthroughs.
5. Mark your progress, record help used, then revisit the box without a guide. Export both JSON and Markdown to keep a copy.

## How readiness works

Every listed prerequisite must be selected, including SQL Injection Fundamentals where the documented route needs it. The current AD module can count as guided practice. Extra topics such as AD Certificate Services, decompilation or binary exploitation remain visible as stretch work.

These are editorial estimates for one documented route, not official HTB unlock requirements or a guarantee that every alternative route fits. A “Starter” label is our learning estimate, not an official HTB difficulty. Module labs remain valuable, especially for basic discovery, pivoting, reporting and the enterprise capstone.

## Keep your accomplishments

The website saves module selections and per-box progress in this browser and site address. It does not update HTB or the original Academy tracker. JSON is the restore format; Markdown is a readable journal for your notebook. Import fills missing box records and adds module selections; existing box records are preserved.

Progress is self-recorded. A guided completion is useful learning; record it honestly and aim for an independent replay. No exam flags or target data are needed for this planner. Do not publish protected assessment answers in your notes.
