## Read this first

Each box is placed under its main practice module. The prerequisite lists refer to a reviewed IppSec route, with extras shown separately. The full route includes every foothold requirement. Initial access may mean a useful domain identity rather than an interactive shell. Labels are editorial learning estimates, not official difficulty. Module completion alone does not cover every technique in every box.

All boxes assume basic methodology, authorized lab scope, and the foundational modules listed in their requirements. Videos contain spoilers and may use old tool versions. Open the [HTB Labs catalogue](https://app.hackthebox.com/machines) and search the exact box name; availability depends on your account.
